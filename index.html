Our Process

Listen
As Innovative Interio is the most reliable turnkey interior designer in Thane, we take special care in delivering what our clients want. For this purpose, we hold a client-designer initial meeting at either's office or site depending on mutual convenience & area location of each. Understand the client's needs & requirements
2
Design
Initial site visits and measurements of the site are conducted. Initial drafting after working on a conceptual design (Plans, Elevations, 3D sketches). Preparation of layout ( 2 optional layouts. Second meeting with a set of drawings for approval of layout (if changes are to be made)
3
Procure
The final meeting will be held with the final layout approved by the client and an interim bill as professional fees for stage 1 work done from our side be it residential interior in Thane or office interior in Thane.
4
Estimate
Preparation of B.O.Q detailed specification. Submission of final quotation with detailed time & payment schedules based on the following scope of work: Civil work, plumbing, electrical, false ceiling. AC, firA.C.alarm. Modular furniture or carpentry woodwork. Light fixtures, blinds, painting, wallpaper. CCTV access control, carpets, P.O.P., woP.O.P.n flooring, chairs, etc.
5
Analysis
Innovative Interio shall provide the final drawings/layout for the premises. Plan, Elevations, 3D sketches with color schemes, Detailed graphics, False ceiling layout, Electrical layout, 2D plan layout, and Elevation layout (2D). Necessary site visit with coordination of contractor & vender as per given schedule.
6
Execute
Supervise on-site for checking the executive as per given drawings/layout to the contractor. Material quality checking. Selection of materials, colors, light fixtures, artwork, accessories, wall coverings, flooring, laminates, etc alongside completing furniture work in Thane. Maintaining the bar schedule for the work progress along with less wastage of materials on-site & keeping the site clean. Our expert professionals and design supervisors will bring any sudden changes made on-site by designers to notice immediately.
