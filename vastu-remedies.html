Vastu Remedies

In today's times, buying a flat that fully complies with Vastu Shastra is almost next to impossible, especially in cities like Mumbai. This means that your lovely home is bound to have some vastu flaws. However, our professionals at Innovative Interio can rectify this to some extent by adopting the various remedies. At Innovative Interio, you can get great interior work in Thane thanks to us; we have staff trained in Vastu principles that can suggest the necessary measures to restore peace and balance to your home. We tackle the vast defects on three fronts:

  • Macro factors: Here, we decide which is the best direction your flat or plot should be facing, the water bodies' position, and any other hurdles or obstructions.
  • Micro factors: Here, we decide what should be the ideal shape of the plot and the need for the presence of cut-offs in flats.
  • Placements of Internal Elements: The position of the kitchen and the master bedroom is very crucial for the progress of the habitat. The best possible arrangements for these two rooms are decided here.

Innovative interio is not only the best interior designer in Thane but also a great civil contractor in Thane to cater to any of your demands. If any of the above factors are not fulfilled by the existing layout of your home, then we will offer the required remedies so that your home is as vastu-compliant as possible.
